# Phase 2 refinement — Less design, more orientation

This refinement preserves the approved shell geometry and changes only visual
priority and component legibility.

- The product header now shares the workspace axis. The signet uses the brand
  accent and a full touch target, creating presence without adding copy.
- Step titles use the official H1-scale token with a firmer weight. Supporting
  copy and eyebrows retain their subordinate roles.
- The workspace is an opaque system surface with one border and one diffuse
  shadow. Removing translucency makes it feel more material with fewer effects.
- Choice rows use the semantic interactive surface. A stronger hover boundary,
  pointer cursor and accent continuation chevron communicate affordance without
  movement or decorative elevation.
- Progress remains outside the card but shares its axis. A four-pixel track and
  compact spacing communicate location without competing with the question.

No DOM order, content, form behavior, calculation or safety behavior changed.
